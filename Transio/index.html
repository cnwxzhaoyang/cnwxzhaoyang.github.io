Built for real file work

One workspace, several protocols.

Two-pane browsing

View local and remote directories side by side, open multiple tabs, and move files with drag-and-drop or the context menu.

Your server connections

Connect to SFTP, FTP, WebDAV, and SMB endpoints you configure. Saved passwords use the system keychain on macOS.

Transfer visibility

Monitor active work in a compact status bar. Open the transfer queue when you need progress, retries, or failure details.

A straightforward flow.

Choose a connection

Add your server details and test the connection before saving it.

Open two locations

Select local folders through the system picker and browse the remote paths you have access to.

Transfer and review

Drag files between panes, then follow the queue or review errors if a transfer needs attention.

Clear about your data.

Transio stores connection profiles and transfer state on your device and sends files to the servers you select. Read the full policy for details about local logs, support emails, and deletion.
