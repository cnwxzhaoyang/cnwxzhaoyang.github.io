Privacy Policy

Know where your files and data go.

Effective October 10, 2026. This policy applies to Transio, published by the Transio developer. Contact: cnwxzhaoyang@163.com.

What Transio stores on your device

Transio saves connection profiles, app preferences, and transfer recovery state locally. Saved server passwords are stored in the macOS Keychain rather than in the app configuration file. Failure records may contain file names, source and destination paths or names, error messages, and timestamps.

Files and remote servers

When you browse or transfer files, Transio connects to the SFTP, FTP, WebDAV, or SMB servers you configure. The server operator may receive file content and related path information. Remote-to-remote copying may pass file data through your device; it is not necessarily a direct server-to-server transfer. You are responsible for choosing and trusting your servers.

Developer services and third parties

Transio does not use a developer-operated backend, analytics service, or crash-reporting service to receive your files, connection profiles, or usage data. Transio does not send these data to the developer by default. Server operators you choose handle the data you send to their servers under their own terms. If you email support, your email provider and our email provider process the message to deliver and answer it. Purchases, if offered through a platform store, are handled by that store under its own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Connection profiles, preferences, and recovery data remain on your device until you change or remove them. Deleting a saved connection removes its saved password from the app's Keychain entry. Use Settings to clear local failure records; ongoing transfers can create new records. Clearing failure records does not delete connections, other app data, or files on your servers. Deleting the app may not remove all application data or Keychain entries automatically; contact us if you need help locating residual data.

Support messages and your choices

If you choose to email us, we use your address and message only to respond to your request. Please redact sensitive paths and never send passwords or private keys. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve your request; you can ask us to delete it by emailing the address below, subject to applicable legal requirements. You can stop a transfer, remove a connection, or clear failure records in the app; you can also revoke local folder access through macOS permissions or by choosing a different folder.

Security and changes

Transfer security depends on the protocol and server you select. SFTP and HTTPS provide transport protection when properly configured; plain FTP or HTTP may not. We may update this policy as Transio changes. The effective date above will be updated when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the Transio developer at cnwxzhaoyang@163.com.
